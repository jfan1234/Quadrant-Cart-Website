FAQs

What is Quadrant Cart?

Quadrant Cart is a mobile coffee and matcha cart serving handcrafted drinks for weddings, private celebrations, corporate events, pop-ups, and community gatherings.

What types of events do you serve?

We serve weddings, showers, birthdays, graduations, office events, brand activations, markets, and private parties. If you want a polished drink experience at your event, we can help build a setup around it.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend reaching out as soon as you have a date, location, and estimated guest count. Weekend dates and wedding season can book quickly, so earlier is always better.

How do I book Quadrant Cart for my event?

Submit the booking form on the Contact Us page with your event details, or email quadrant.cart@gmail.com. We’ll follow up with availability, package recommendations, and next steps within 24-48 hours.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve the Twin Cities area and surrounding suburbs. Events outside the immediate metro may be possible depending on the date, distance, and event needs.

What is included with the cart setup?

Each booking includes the mobile cart, drink preparation, service staff, setup, breakdown, and the selected drink menu. Some packages also include custom drinks, custom cups, and cart signage.

Can we customize the menu?

Yes. We offer a standard menu and can also work with you on custom drinks, signature flavors, and event-specific touches depending on the package and timeline.

Do you offer both coffee and matcha?

Yes. Our menu includes coffee, matcha, matcha lattes, specialty limited drops, and milk options including whole and oat milk.

How much space does the cart need?

We need a clean, level setup area with enough room for the cart, team, and guest flow. Once we know your venue and guest count, we can help confirm the best placement.

Do you need access to power or water?

Power access is helpful for most events. Water needs may vary based on the venue and package, so we’ll confirm setup requirements during the booking process.

Can the cart be used outdoors?

Outdoor events may be possible if the setup area is level, protected from weather, and has the needed access for service. We’ll review the event details with you before confirming.

Can you accommodate dairy-free guests?

Yes. We offer oat milk and can discuss drink options for guests with dairy-free preferences. Please note any allergy or dietary needs when submitting your inquiry.
